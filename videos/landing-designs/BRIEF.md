## Visual & Motion Architecture

- Curved 3D amphitheater / multi-plane spatial gallery with realistic perspective (`perspective: 1400px`) and deep multi-layer parallax.
- Deep obsidian and luxury dark violet-slate background (`#08080c`, `#100e18`) with ambient soft radial glow beams and fine grain overlay.
- Beveled glass planes with subtle border sheen, deep multi-stop drop shadows, and soft ambient reflections.
- Mirrored velocity curves (`power2.inOut`, `power3.out`) with dynamic rack-focus motion blur on transitions.
- Zero idle wobble; continuous intentional camera choreography.
- Exactly 12.0 seconds duration.
