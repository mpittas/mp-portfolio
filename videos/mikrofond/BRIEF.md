# Brief: Mikrofond Homepage 3D Orbital Showcase

## Concept
A cinematic, clean 3D orbital camera exploration of the official homepage of Mikrofond (https://www.mikrofond.bg/). The video focuses 100% on the homepage itself without any artificial text overlays or badges.

## Creative Direction
- **Stage**: Atmospheric dark studio backdrop with deep midnight navy (`#06101e`) and radiant cyan (`#00a8cc`) volumetric glow matching Mikrofond's brand identity.
- **Surface**: Floating UI plate with elevation shadow, rounded corners, subtle specular sheen reflecting camera motion, and delicate depth of field.
- **Camera Movement**: Multi-axis orbital trajectory (pitch, yaw, roll, z-depth) with velocity-matched transitions and continuous organic Lissajous drift.
- **Pacing**:
  - 0.0s – 2.8s: Cinematic reveal and establishing orbit over the hero and financial offerings.
  - 2.8s – 5.5s: Orbital swoop and glide down through value pillars and business partner showcase.
  - 5.5s – 8.2s: Deep orbital glide through quality certificates and impact statistics ("Микрофонд в цифри").
  - 8.2s – 12.0s: The Grand Zoom-Out finale — camera pulls back into wide 3D space to capture the complete homepage UI in all its structural beauty.
