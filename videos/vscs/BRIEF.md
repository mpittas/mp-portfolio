## Intent

High-fidelity unnarrated 3D spatial camera showcase of `https://www.vscs-bg.com`. Features authentic UI captured directly from the live bilingual application across its core pages:
1. Home: Hero "Вашият партньор в соларния бизнес", client trust logos, turnkey solutions intro, and key statistics.
2. About Us (/za-nas): Renewable energy vision, founders journey, and core values (Професионализъм, Иновации, Устойчивост).
3. Services (/uslugi): Full-lifecycle EPC engineering (Проектиране, Доставка, Строителни работи, Електромеханични работи).
4. Projects (/proekti): Completed utility-scale photovoltaic solar plants (Полинеза, Златното пиле, Старата Мелница, Черногорово, Зволен).
5. Contact & Consultation (/kontakti): Free consultation booking and corporate presence.

## Customizations

- Distinct curved 3D amphitheater layout: pages positioned with true spatial depth (`translateZ`, `rotateY`, `rotateX`) creating dynamic multi-layer parallax as the camera flies between them.
- Smooth velocity-matched camera curves with mirrored `power2.inOut` and `power3.out` transitions, dynamic rack-focus motion blur, and organic micro-drift.
- Signature VSCS clean-energy dark theme: deep forest obsidian (`#001D13`, `#022D1E`, `#051511`) with glowing emerald accents (`#00966E`, `#00D689`) and soft ambient solar lighting.
- Exactly 10.0 seconds.
