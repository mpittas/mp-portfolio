## Intent
High-fidelity unnarrated 3D camera showcase of `https://digio-seven.vercel.app` (Digio):
1. Hero Landing: "Designing the Next Generation of Brands" with dynamic 3D sphere and bold typography.
2. Selected Work Showcase: High-end agency portfolio cards and device mockups.
3. Services Suite: Comprehensive branding, mobile, and web design offerings.
4. Membership & Pricing: Subscription tiers and closing call to action.

## Creative Direction
- Dynamic 3D perspective (`perspective: 1450px`) with smooth orbital camera arcs (`rotateY`, `rotateX`, `rotateZ`), continuous organic Lissajous micro-drift, and rack-focus velocity blur.
- Signature Digio palette: deep obsidian carbon `#06050a`, radiant crimson glow `#e11d48`, electric violet accents `#9333ea`, and crisp frosted edge borders.
- Pacing:
  - 0.0s – 2.8s: Establishing reveal & orbit over Hero branding card.
  - 2.8s – 5.8s: Orbital swoop into the Selected Work portfolio showcase.
  - 5.8s – 8.6s: Spatial glide through Services and proof metrics.
  - 8.6s – 12.0s: Grand pull-back and settle onto the full architectural showcase.
- Duration: 12.0 seconds (360 frames @ 30fps).
