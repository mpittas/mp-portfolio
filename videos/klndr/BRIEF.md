## Intent
High-fidelity unnarrated 3D camera showcase of `https://klndr-v1.vercel.app` (klndr):
1. Hero Landing: Clean introduction to the visual day planner and value proposition.
2. Core Planner Experience: Detailed deep-dive into the live time-blocking interface with snaps, stretchable blocks, categories, and routine checklists.
3. Steps & Workflow: Visual breakdown of how users pick templates, drop activities onto the timeline, and maintain daily flow.
4. Features & Architecture: Highlighting keyboard shortcuts, month view synchronization, and cross-device Firebase persistence.
5. Finale: Seamless grand pull-back into full perspective showing the refined design system.

## Creative Direction
- Dynamic 3D perspective (`perspective: 1450px`) with smooth orbital camera arcs (`rotateY`, `rotateX`, `rotateZ`), continuous organic Lissajous micro-drift, and rack-focus velocity blur.
- Color palette tailored to klndr: deep indigo midnight backdrop (`#070a14`), electric violet and royal blue ambient glows (`#6366f1`, `#3b82f6`, `#8b5cf6`), clean modern cards with crisp edge borders and specular sheens.
- Pacing:
  - 0.0s – 2.8s: Establishing reveal & orbit over Hero landing and headline ("Shape your day by dragging blocks onto a timeline").
  - 2.8s – 6.0s: Signature orbital swoop into the live Day Planner interface, exploring the quarter-hour grid, scheduled blocks, and routines.
  - 6.0s – 8.8s: Smooth travel across Steps & Workflow and Comparison modules.
  - 8.8s – 12.0s: The Grand Finale pull-back into wide 3D space showcasing the entire multi-card system and final CTA.
- Duration: 12.0 seconds (360 frames @ 30fps).
