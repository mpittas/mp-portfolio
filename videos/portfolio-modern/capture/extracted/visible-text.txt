[div] A motion-first freelance folio I built in Vue 3 and TypeScript, so the site itself is the work sample. You land on an SVG wipe loader, then a split-text hero that scales down as you scroll. Services stack as sticky cards. Selected works keep a giant index that ticks as you pass each project, and the WebM previews only play and un-blur when they enter the viewport.

The motion layer is GSAP: ScrollTrigger, character-level text, elastic magnetic buttons, and a custom cursor that becomes a view chip over the work list. Tailwind v4 holds the flax-smoke palette and a fluid type scale. Lenis handles the scroll. A grain overlay sits over the page. There is also an infinite role marquee, a testimonial slider, and a full-viewport contact plate on a looping video with a Lottie globe.
