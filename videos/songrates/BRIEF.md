## Intent

High-fidelity unnarrated 3D camera montage of `https://songrates.vercel.app`. Features authentic UI captured directly from the live application across its core inner pages:
1. Home Feed: Hero "Discover and Rate Music" with live audio previews and Trending Songs list.
2. Explore Page: Deep music discovery, category search, genre chips, and curated charts.
3. Album Scoring Experience: Track-by-track rating interface, community score chips, and review cards.
4. Curated Playlists & Profiles: Apple Music Top 100 global playlists and user profile leveling system.

## Customizations

- Genuine application UI screenshots across 4+ inner pages.
- Dynamic 3D perspective (`perspective: 1450px`) with smooth orbital camera arcs (`rotateY`, `rotateX`, `rotateZ`), continuous organic micro-drift, and velocity blur.
- Signature Songrates vinyl dark theme: deep coral-to-wine radial glow (`#FF7F5F`, `#8F1D28`, `#0F0C11`) and emerald rating badges (`#4FBF82`).
